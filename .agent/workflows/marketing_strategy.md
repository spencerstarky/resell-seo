# ResellSEO: Go-to-Market Strategy

## Persona 1: "The Drowning Volume Seller" (The Pro)
**Profile:** Full-time reseller. Owns a warehouse or storage unit. Has a "Death Pile" of 500+ unlisted items. Uses a cross-listing tool like Vendoo/ListPerfectly but finds it slow.
- **Pain Point:** Throughput. They can't list fast enough. They hate the manual click-work of eBay.
- **Why they need you:** "Push All Live" and "Batch Optimization". They don't care about a 2% score increase; they care about clearing 50 items in 5 minutes.
- **Marketing Hook:** *"Turn your Death Pile into Cash. Audit and Optimize 100 listings in one click."*
- **Channel:** LinkedIn, YouTube Integration sponsorships (Reseller vloggers), eBay Seller Conferences.

## Persona 2: "The Side Hustle Newbie" (The Learner)
**Profile:** Teacher/Nurse/Student flipping items on weekends. Lists 10-20 items a week. Anxious about "doing it right".
- **Pain Point:** Uncertainty. *"Why aren't my items selling?"* *"Am I using the right keywords?"* They feel invisible.
- **Why they need you:** **The SEO Scorecard**. They need validation. They want the "Green Checkmark" to tell them they did a good job.
- **Marketing Hook:** *"Stop Guessing. Get an instant SEO Score for every listing and fix your mistakes automatically."*
- **Channel:** TikTok ("Check your eBay Score!"), Instagram Reels, Facebook Reseller Groups.

## Persona 3: "The Niche Curator" (The Expert)
**Profile:** Sells high-end Vintage, Sneakers, or Tech to a specific audience. Knows their niche perfectly (e.g., knows "Single Stitch" or "Gore-Tex").
- **Pain Point:** Burnout & Repetition. They know *what* to write, but typing "Mens Vintage 90s Made in USA..." 100 times is exhausting. They are afraid AI will "ruin" their carefully curated titles (hence the fear of hallucinations).
- **Why they need you:** **Safe Keyword Injection**. They can trust your tool to add the "boring" keywords (Size, Laptop, Travel) while they focus on the high-value sourcing.
- **Marketing Hook:** *"The AI that respects your expertise. Safe keyword expansion that never hallucinates."*
- **Channel:** Sub-reddits (r/Flipping), specialized Discord communities, Twitter/X.

## Action Plan
1.  **Feature Flagging:** Consider marketing the "Push All" button heavily to Persona 1 (Pro Plan).
2.  **Free Tool:** Release a free "Title Grader" (Input 1 title -> Get Score) to capture Persona 2 emails.
3.  **Trust Signals:** Publish case studies on the "Hallucination Filter" to win over Persona 3.
